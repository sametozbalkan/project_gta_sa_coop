CoopTunnel carries CoopAndreas' traffic over Steam (Spacewar, AppID 480).
No port forwarding needed; it works behind CGNAT too.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Steam running and signed in.
- The two players are Steam friends (the host refuses everyone else).

1) CONNECTION TEST (no game needed)
- Host (the one who runs the server): starts "1-Host.bat" and keeps its window open.
- Friend: starts "2-Test-connection.bat". It finds the host by itself, sends 20 pings and shows
  the ping and the route (direct or through a Valve relay).

2) PLAYING
- Host: CoopAndreas Server + "1-Host.bat" + the game. Connect screen: 127.0.0.1, port 6767.
- Friend: "3-Join.bat" + the game. Connect screen: 127.0.0.1, port 6767.
  Your nickname must differ from the host's. The host connects first: the first player to connect
  runs the missions, unless the server's server-config.ini names someone in story-host.

NOTES
- Steam shows you as "playing Spacewar"; that's normal.
- If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
- If the firewall asks, allow "Private networks".
